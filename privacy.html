Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026

Summary

DrPlantes is a local-only application. All your data — plants, photos, watering reminders — is stored exclusively on your device. We have no servers, we do not create user accounts, and we do not send any information to the Internet or to third parties.

What the app stores on your device

  • Information about your plants (name, species, location, acquisition date, notes).
  • Photos of plants that you add.
  • Watering calendar events (scheduled, completed, postponed).
  • App preferences.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not have user accounts or our own servers.
  • We do not send data to the Internet or to cloud services.
  • We do not collect personal identifiers (email, name, phone, location).
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

Device permissions

The app requests the following permissions only to provide its functionality:

  • Camera: to take photos of your plants (photos are stored on your device).
  • Photos / Gallery: to select existing images and associate them with your plants.

These permissions can be managed at any time from your operating system settings.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored in the app's internal storage on your device and is not shared with other apps. You can delete it at any time via "Erase all data" on the Settings screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Backups

DrPlantes does not back up data to the cloud. If you uninstall the app or change devices, you will lose all your data.

Children

The app does not collect any personal data. Nevertheless, we recommend adult supervision for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how we handle data, we will update this policy and indicate a new effective date.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: contact@drplantes.app.
